General Discharge Guidance

Patients should follow the instructions provided by their healthcare professional.
Patients should take medicines according to the prescribed instructions and should not independently change the dose.
Patients should attend recommended follow-up appointments.
Patients should contact their healthcare provider if they experience worsening symptoms or have concerns about their recovery.
For severe or life-threatening symptoms, patients should seek emergency medical care.
Discharge instructions should be explained in language that patients and caregivers can understand.
