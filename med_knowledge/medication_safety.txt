Medication Safety

Patients should take medicines exactly as prescribed.
Patients should not change medication doses without speaking with their healthcare professional.
Patients should keep a current list of medicines.
Patients should inform healthcare professionals about medication allergies.
If a patient is unsure about how to take a medicine, they should contact their healthcare professional or pharmacist.
Medication instructions should clearly communicate the medicine name, dose and frequency when those details are available in the patient record.
